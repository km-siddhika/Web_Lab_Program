Flexbox or Grid?

Both are useful. The choice depends on the layout you need.

Flexbox

Best for arranging items in one direction: a row or a column.

LogoAboutContact

Grid

Best when rows and columns need to work together.

Quick guide

Use Flexbox for a navigation bar or a row of buttons. Use Grid for a dashboard, gallery or page with clear rows and columns. They can also be combined.
